# SWGOH core mechanics

## Battles
- Squads are 5 characters (3 in 3v3 Grand Arena). Only the leader's Leader ability is active.
- Fleet battles use a capital ship, up to 3 starting ships and up to 4 reinforcements.
- Units act when their Turn Meter reaches 100%. Speed controls how fast it fills. Gain/Remove Turn Meter effects shift that timing directly.
- Special abilities have cooldowns counted in the owner's turns. Basic abilities have none.
- Protection absorbs damage before Health, and a unit is defeated at 0 Health.
- Damage is Physical or Special, reduced by the target's Armor or Resistance respectively. True damage ignores defenses.
- Critical hits deal Critical Damage (base 150%). Critical Avoidance reduces the chance of being crit.
- Potency improves the chance to land debuffs, and Tenacity improves the chance to resist them. Effects that "can't be resisted" always land unless evaded or blocked.
- Galactic Legends (GLs) are endgame units with an Ultimate ability that is fuelled by Ultimate Charge. Some effects exclude GLs or raid bosses.
- Battles have a time limit, typically 5 minutes.

## Status effects (general meaning; a kit's own wording takes precedence)
- Buffs are positive and debuffs negative. Dispel removes them unless they "can't be dispelled". Buff Immunity blocks gaining buffs.
- Taunt: enemies must target this unit. Stealth: this unit can't be targeted while an ally without Stealth is available.
- Stun: miss the next turn. Fear and Daze also restrict actions, and Daze prevents counters, assists and bonus Turn Meter.
- Ability Block: can't use abilities other than the Basic.
- Healing Immunity: can't recover Health or Protection.
- Foresight: evade the next enemy attack.
- Expose: the next attack against this unit deals bonus damage, then Expose is removed.
- Stat buffs and debuffs (Offense, Defense, Speed, Critical Chance, Critical Damage, Potency, Tenacity, Evasion, Health Up/Down) modify that stat while active.
- Counter: attack back when attacked. Assist: attack alongside an ally. Bonus Turn: act immediately again.
- Revive: return a defeated unit to battle. Instant defeat/Destroy removes a unit regardless of Health.

## Progression
- Gear tiers go up to Gear 13, after which Relic tiers add large stat gains. Stars (1-7) gate abilities and power.
- Zeta upgrades unlock stronger versions of key abilities (marked [Zeta]).
- Omicron upgrades only take effect in specific game modes (marked [Omicron: <mode>]).
- Mods: 6 slots, each with a primary stat, secondaries and a set bonus. Sets include Speed, Offense, Critical Damage, Critical Chance, Potency, Tenacity, Health and Defense. Speed secondaries are usually the most valued.
- Datacrons are account-level items granting stat and faction/character bonuses for the current set.

## Game modes
- Squad Arena and Fleet Arena: asynchronous PvP against defensive squads, where the arena leader matters.
- Grand Arena Championships (GAC): 3v3 or 5v5 matchups where players set defenses and attack the opponent's.
- Territory Wars (TW): guild vs guild, with defensive squads placed in territories.
- Territory Battles (TB): guild PvE across planets, with combat missions that often require specific factions.
- Raids: guild PvE boss fights scored by damage.
- Conquest and Galactic Challenges: rotating PvE events with feats and modifiers.
